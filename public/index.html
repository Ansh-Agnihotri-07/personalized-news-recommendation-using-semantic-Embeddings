PROJECT 41 · UNDER THE HOOD

Meaning makes the connection.

Keyword matching can miss the idea behind a phrase. Semantic embeddings help bridge that gap.

01

Describe your interests

Your selected topics and description become the starting point for your profile.

02

Represent the meaning

MiniLM encodes each interest and article into a 384-dimensional vector. Related concepts tend to be closer together.

03

Find the connection

Cosine similarity compares your profile with each article. The most similar articles appear first.

04

Make it more personal

When you like stories, your profile blends 80% stated interests with 20% liked-article meaning.

What does the score mean?

Cosine similarity ranges from −1 to 1. A higher value indicates closer embedding directions, not a probability, accuracy score, or guarantee that you will like a story. Explanations show the closest individual interest; ranking uses your combined profile.

Model: Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 · Mean pooling · L2 normalization · Local CPU inference. The first run downloads model files; your interest text is processed on this computer. Saved stories and preferences live in this browser. Sample articles are fictional educational content.

Semantic scores use cosine similarity. Keyword scores show the fraction of unique query terms found in an article’s title, summary, or tags, excluding common stop words. These scales are different and are not accuracy percentages. Zero keyword scores are tied; their order is arbitrary.
